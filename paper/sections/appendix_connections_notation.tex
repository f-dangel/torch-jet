In this work we defined the entities of the propagation scheme to be 
\begin{equation*}
\vf_k \coloneqq \frac{\mathrm{d}^k}{\mathrm{d}t^k} \vf(\vx(t)) |_{t=0},
\end{equation*}
which results in the jet and Faà di Bruno scaled by the factor $\nicefrac{1}{k!}$.
If the factor is part of the coefficient we get the following
\begin{equation*}
\Tilde{\vf}_k \coloneqq \frac{1}{k!} \frac{\mathrm{d}^k}{\mathrm{d}t^k} \vf(\vx(t)) |_{t=0}.
\end{equation*}
Analogously, the input jet's coefficients carry the same factor, $\Tilde{\vx}_s \coloneqq \nicefrac{1}{s!}\, \vx_s$ with $\vx_s \coloneqq \frac{\mathrm{d}^s}{\mathrm{d}t^s} \vx(t) |_{t=0}$, \ie the input jet $\Tilde{\vx}$ is scaled as well.
The jet is then given as
\begin{align*}
  \Tilde{J}^K \vf : \sR \to \sR^C\,,
  \quad (\Tilde{J}^K \vf)(t) := \sum_{k=0}^K\Tilde{\vf}_k t^k. 
\end{align*}
and the Faà di Bruno formula (\cref{eq:faa-di-bruno}) changes to
\begin{align}
  \label{eq:faa-di-bruno-scaled}
  % \textstyle % Comment out this line if we have enough space
  \Tilde{\vf}_k
  =
  \sum_{\sigma \in \partitioning(k)}
  \Tilde{\nu}(\sigma)
  \left<
  \partial^{|\sigma|} \vf,
  \tensorprod{s \in \sigma} \Tilde{\vx}_s
  \right>
  \quad
  \text{with}
  \quad
  \Tilde{\nu}(\sigma)
  =
  \frac{1}{
    \left(
      \prod_{s \in \sigma
      }
      n_s!
    \right)
  }\,.
\end{align}
The prefactor $\nicefrac{1}{k!}$ was cancelled out with $k!$ from the nominator of
$\nu(\sigma)$ and the factor $\nicefrac{1}{\prod_{s \in \sigma} s!}$ is comsumed by the different $\tilde{\vx}_s$.
In this notation, we have to rescale the propagation's output to obtain the derivative $\vf_k = k! \Tilde{\vf}_k$.
